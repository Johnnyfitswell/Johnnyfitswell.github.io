OUR ARTIST

A welcoming experience matters. Here’s the person behind the work.

johnny.jpg

Johnny Fitswell

"I'm an artist of all sorts, down to the bone, through blood sweat and tears. Always watching, always evolving, aware of change. Trying not to have a style while having a style. Art will create itself in its rawest nature."

Johnny started tattooing at 25 and quickly made a name for his color work.

Known for vibrant, clean designs, he’s not limited to one style. Whether it’s color, black and grey, or something custom — he adapts and builds each piece around the client.

His approach is simple: understand the vision, bring it to life, and make sure every client walks out with something they’re proud to wear.
