Our Artist

A welcoming experience matters. Here’s the person behind the work — friendly, focused, and ready to bring your idea to life.

Johnny Fitswell

Color Watercolor Any Style Consult-First
“I'm an artist of all sorts, down to the bone, through blood sweat and tears. Always watching, always evolving, aware of change. Trying not to have a style while having a style. Art will create itself in its rawest nature.”

Johnny started tattooing at 25 and quickly built a name for bold, vibrant color work.

He’s best known for clean, punchy color and watercolor-style tattoos — but he’s versatile and comfortable working across any style.

His approach is simple: listen first, design with purpose, and deliver clean work you’ll be proud to wear — friendly throughout, confident, and focused on getting it done right.
